Stonkalicious
A dynamic NFT on Robinhood Chain. Each NFT is a warrior that starts naive and becomes a hardened elite as the owner’s bag performs.
The flex is the visual. The warrior is a living status symbol for how well you are doing on-chain.

What it is
Every NFT is the same character at different stages of power:

Naive — young, open face, light armor, soft purple energy
Focused — tighter, more armor, still no helmet, energy turning toward fire
Hardened — practical battle helmet, tougher look, real fire starting
Elite — battle-worn warrior, bright flames, aura

Style: clean Azuki / manga. Male ego fantasy. Starts a bit silly. Ends serious. Always holding a magical orb. No text on the image.

What it reacts to
The NFT does not hold assets. It reads the current owner’s wallet.
Counts:

Official Robinhood tokenized stocks and ETFs
A small fixed list of large Robinhood Chain coins (only a few names, chosen by you)

Does not count:

Random memes
Stablecoins
Anything not on the allowlist

Scoring:

Current portfolio strength from allowlisted balances
Growth since this owner acquired the NFT
Allowlisted coins can count, but are capped so a meme pump cannot instantly create a Tier 4 warrior

On transfer:
Snapshot resets. New owner starts fresh. The warrior can drop tiers. That is the point.

How it works

Someone mints or buys the NFT.
At that moment, record owner, time, and starting bag value.
When the NFT is viewed, look up the current owner, read their allowlisted balances, compare to the snapshot, pick the tier, return the matching image and attributes.
If it transfers, write a new snapshot for the new owner.

A public leaderboard ranks warriors by current strength + growth.

Why people would care
Young men already battle at a screen. This gives that fight a body.

Competition: visible hierarchy
Creation / identity: this is my warrior
Virality: Tier 1 vs Tier 4 is instantly shareable
Fairness: you cannot buy a maxed warrior and keep the glow after selling it

This is different from projects where the NFT is a vault that holds stocks. Here the NFT is a mirror of the owner.

What v1 is not
No AI agents.
No extra token.
No ERC-6551 stock vaults.
No complex game world.
No scoring the entire wallet.
v1 is only: warrior visuals + allowlisted holdings + growth since acquisition + reset on transfer + simple leaderboard.

How to build it (any tool)
Work in stages. Do not dump the whole product into one prompt.
Stage 1 — Rules
Lock the stock list, the 3–5 extra coins, scoring, tier thresholds, transfer reset, leaderboard.
Stage 2 — Art
Lock the 4-tier character sheet and image prompts.
Stage 3 — Architecture
ERC-721 + off-chain metadata server + small database for snapshots + simple site (mint, my warrior, leaderboard).
Stage 4 — Metadata
JSON shape, attributes, caching, empty-wallet and transfer edge cases.
Stage 5 — Build
First make mint + live tier from balances. Then add snapshots and growth. Then leaderboard and polish.
Stage 6 — Audience
Show the transformation. One sentence: Your bag grows your warrior. Main surface is X.
